A make.fun entry for [One Line, One Laugh: Roast Your Fridge](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-one-laugh-roast-your-fridge-f1063521): one line, at most 200 characters, roasting the most boring object in the kitchen.

## The line

> My fridge's water dispenser has two settings: a polite trickle, and a fire hose with a personal vendetta against my shirt.

## How it runs

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `entry.txt` under `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or `./output` when unset). Stdlib only, no network, no secrets.
